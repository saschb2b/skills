# Game design knowledge behind the game-design skill.

The procedure lives in the skill's SKILL.md. These concepts hold the depth, grouped into three areas: the design core (how to read and design mechanics), the interface layer (how the player perceives and operates the game), and the concept area (how a game's identity is captured, questioned, and tended as a living OKF bundle). The catalog and principles are distilled from a personal knowledge base of 17 dissected games; the frameworks, systems, and interface notes are grounded in the standard design and UX literature, cited inline.

# Areas

- [The design core](design/) - 7 concepts. The dissection method, the cross-game pattern catalog, the design moves worth stealing, the established frameworks, systems and economies and balance, the critique playbook, and inventory and items.
- [The interface layer](interface/) - 8 concepts. UX foundations, menus and screens and the HUD, transitions, onboarding and tutorials, dialogue and narrative UI, guidance and feedback, input prompts, and accessibility.
- [The concept area](concept/) - 4 concepts. The game concept bundle (the OKF home of a game's identity), the seeding interview, the tending lifecycle, and the copy-paste templates.
