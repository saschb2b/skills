# Background concepts behind the comment-stinky taxonomy, catalog, and write gate.

- [The Comment's Real Audience](comment-audience.md) - The reader holds only the file, never the diff, and every rule in the skill follows from that asymmetry.
- [Agent Context Collapse](agent-context-collapse.md) - Why coding agents write comments about the edit rather than the code, structurally rather than carelessly.
- [The What, How, and Why Ladder](why-not-what.md) - The three rungs a comment can address, which one is worth a line, and the narrow cases where a what-comment is right.
- [Routing Information to Its Real Home](information-routing.md) - The homes a fact can live in, ranked by whether the system notices when it goes wrong, and why a comment is the fallback.
- [Comment Decay](comment-decay.md) - Why comments drift into being false, which kinds rot fastest, and the structural defenses that slow it.
- [Interface Comments vs Implementation Comments](interface-vs-implementation.md) - The two populations, their different readers, and the three failures of mixing them.
- [Non-Obvious Information](nonobvious-information.md) - The test for whether a comment should exist at all, and how to find the ones that are missing.
- [Commit Message Craft](commit-message-craft.md) - The destination for everything the write gate strips out, and why it holds change information better than the source file.
