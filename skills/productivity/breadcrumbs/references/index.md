# Breadcrumbs reference knowledge

Use these concepts selectively after a real trace exposes avoidable understanding friction. They help diagnose and repair the codebase; they are not a template for creating a breadcrumb system in the consuming repository.

- [Knowledge smell catalog](smells.md) - Observable signals, likely causes, false positives, and preferred repair directions.
- [Repair patterns](repairs.md) - Concrete transformations and a placement guide for code, tests, comments, errors, and existing documentation.
- [Scope and autonomy rails](scope-rails.md) - Confidence, blast-radius, and verification rules for deciding whether to heal now or surface the issue.
- [Worked healing examples](examples.md) - End-to-end traces showing the difference between recording a finding and repairing its cause.
- [Research basis](evidence.md) - Primary research and authoritative engineering guidance translated into bounded rules for the skill.
